# Audiory Producer Subscription Tiers

Choose the ideal plan to scale your music production marketplace presence globally. We support secure upgrades via **M-Pesa** and **PayPal**.

## 1. Free Plan ($0/mo)
* **Upload Limits:** Basic beat distribution slots.
* **Store Access:** Standard checkout layout.
* **Core Benefit:** Start building your audience risk-free.

## 2. Starter Plan ($15/mo)
* **Upload Limits:** Higher limit for track uploads.
* **Features:** Access to standard distribution assets and customized profile views.
* **Payment Gateways:** Pay via card processing or instantaneous local mobile transactions.

## 3. Pro Plan ($39/mo)
* **Upload Limits:** Bulk uploading tool activated.
* **Features:** Advanced internal analytics engines, customized marketing funnels, and unlimited private messaging capabilities.

## 4. Elite Plan ($65/mo)
* **Upload Limits:** Unrestricted premium capacity footprint.
* **Features:** Full global marketplace visibility, Content ID management access, custom domain linking, and prioritized operational developer support.
