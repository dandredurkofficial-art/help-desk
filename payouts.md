# Payments & Payouts Guide

At Audiory, we make sure our producers keep the absolute maximum amount of their hard-earned money with ultra-low transactional fees.

## When do payouts happen?
Payout processing runs dynamically. Once a buyer successfully completes a transaction on the marketplace, funds are routed safely according to your linked distribution methods.

## Verification Requirements
To comply with physical address and tax rules, ensure you have submitted:
* A valid government ID.
* A clear bank statement or official document confirming your residency status.
