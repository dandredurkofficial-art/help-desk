# Payments & Payout Systems

Audiory offers optimized payment configurations for creators globally and locally inside East Africa.

## Buying Marketplace Items
Producers and buyers can run transactions securely using:
* **M-Pesa Express STK Push:** Direct verification using mobile infrastructure.
* **PayPal:** Instant secure credit check processing for standard currencies.

## Withdrawing Earnings (Payouts)
When a buyer completes an order, funds route safely to your creator wallet balance.
1. Go to your **Wallet Dashboard**.
2. Submit a **Payout Request**.
3. Select your destination pathway (**PayPal** or **M-Pesa** phone lines).
4. Admin verification takes less than 24 hours to clear.
