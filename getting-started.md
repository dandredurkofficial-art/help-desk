# Getting Started with Audiory

Welcome to the Audiory Help Desk! This guide will help you set up your profile and navigate the marketplace seamlessly.

## 1. Setting Up Your Profile
* Log in to your main Audiory account.
* Navigate to your **Dashboard Settings**.
* Upload a high-quality profile banner and avatar.

## 2. Setting Up Your Producer Shop
To sell your beats globally, ensure your payout details are fully updated in your main user portal before uploading audio tracks.
