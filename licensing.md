# Beat Licensing Options

Audiory offers transparent legal contracts to protect both music producers and independent artists.

## 1. Non-Exclusive Licenses
Perfect for artists working on a budget. The producer maintains full ownership of the master recording and can resell the same beat to other creators.

## 2. Exclusive Rights
When an artist purchases an exclusive license, the beat is completely removed from the Audiory catalog and cannot be sold to anyone else. Custom contract terms can be dictated straight from your control panel.
