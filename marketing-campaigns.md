# Dashboard Marketing Campaigns

Boost traffic to your profile page and sound kits by running automated ad banners on Audiory.

## How to Set Up a Campaign
1. Go to the **Marketing** tab inside your Producer Dashboard.
2. Select your targeted promotional criteria (e.g., specific beat links or structural banners).
3. Budget allocations can be funded seamlessly using **M-Pesa** or **PayPal**.
4. Track performance in real-time with impressions, preview plays, and immediate conversation rates on your metrics line.
